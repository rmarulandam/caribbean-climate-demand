# Caribbean climate and residential electricity demand

Status: CURRENT WORKING POSITION; exploratory; no FROZEN DECISION.

## Initial proposal

The nine-page Spanish proposal, *Clima horario y consumo eléctrico residencial en el
Caribe colombiano* (August 2026), asks a causal question (pp. 2–3), sketches climate
randomization as an ideal experiment (p. 6), and proposes a subscriber–billing-cycle
panel using within-unit weather variation and municipality/subscriber and month-year
fixed effects (p. 8). Increased consumption under hot/humid conditions is a hypothesis.
The mechanism diagram (p. 4) includes housing, appliances, income, tariffs and outages.

SUI billing TC2/Formato 2, user TC1/Formato 1 and ERA5-Land are called confirmed in the
proposal (p. 7). Access, usable fields, continuity and linkage have NOT been verified
here. NSRDB/equivalent solar data and operator GIS are conditional; linked dwelling data
are ideal. No sample or empirical results were found. The initial output is advanced-
econometrics course research; this PDF establishes no target-journal commitment.

## Working scope

What is the short-run causal response of residential electricity per subscriber-day to hourly temperature, humidity and solar-radiation exposure over the billing cycle in the Colombian Caribbean?

Audit access, identifiers, billing dates, geography, missingness and coverage before choosing sample, exposure reference, functional form, fixed effects, clusters and heterogeneity.
